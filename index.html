About me

Hello, my name is Timothy, known as “Augke”. I’m a videographer and filmmaker in Rotterdam, and I study Audiovisual Design at the Willem de Kooning Academy.

I focus on concert films, music videos and social media content. I work with artists, event organisers and creative teams to capture their energy and vision on film.

I’ve worked with a wide range of artists on live shows, music videos and events, in the Netherlands and abroad. From filming and directing to editing and colour grading, I enjoy being involved in every stage of the creative process.

Open to new projects and collaborations, in any format and at any location.
